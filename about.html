About

The College

Bowles Hall is a small, diverse community within UC Berkeley, designed to help residents learn to embrace challenges and seek solutions to the problems they will face in life beyond Berkeley. It is the only residential college on campus, and a University home for the whole of a student's undergraduate years.

I

Advising and guiding

Academic and career advising sit at the center of the Bowles experience. Led by the in-residence College Principal, a senior member of the Berkeley faculty, and supported by a Board of distinguished faculty, administrators and alumni, it helps students find their aptitudes and interests and pursue a degree and career to match.

II

A genuinely diverse home

The college welcomes people of all backgrounds, identities and academic interests. Bringing those talents and passions together in a stable community lets residents learn from peers unlike themselves, and the Hall is built to be a home for several years, not one.

III

Run by its students

We promote individual and collective achievement in a culture of integrity and respect. Residents elect officers, maintain civil discipline, and form the committees that run the Hall's social, intramural and outreach life.

Live-in Faculty

Mentors who live down the hall.

Our residential college experience is guided by a professional team that lives in residence alongside students. Working closely with the Bowles Hall Foundation Board of Directors and an active alumni group, they help residents stay on track academically, prepare for advanced study and careers, and become meaningful contributors to society.

They live and dine among students, and meet them at meals, performances, sporting events and in the hallways. Sometimes they're even in the same class.

Who runs Bowles

Alumni-led, and independent.

Bowles Hall is operated by the Bowles Hall Foundation, a non-profit, under a 45-year ground lease with the University of California. Its $45 million renovation was funded by private revenue bonds, with no money from the University or California taxpayers. The Foundation works closely with the campus, and its Board includes Cal faculty and administrators alongside alumni.

The Bowles Hall Association is the Hall's student government, and the Student President sits on the Foundation's Board.
