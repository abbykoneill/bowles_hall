Giving

Support the next generation

When the Bowles Hall project began in 2005, it was a small group of alumni determined to revive the residential college. In 2020, the first class of four-year residents graduated from the restored Hall.

Help us sustain the Bowles experience and give a new generation the programs and traditions that make it meaningful. The Bowles Hall Foundation is a 501(c)(3), and gifts are tax-deductible.

Naming opportunities · $10,000 and up

Fund a fellow or a program

Annual gifts fund a position or program for one year. An endowment can be named for you or someone you choose and, continually replenished by investment returns, supports the Hall permanently.

Student Fellows are experienced residents, chosen competitively, who help design the college's academic and career advising, hold office hours, lead conflict mediation, and support students from diverse backgrounds.
