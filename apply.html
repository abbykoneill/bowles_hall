How selection works

Read by students, judged on your words

  1. You apply

    Along with your essays, you share your living-style preferences, like cleanliness, quiet and bedtime, and can name roommates you'd like.

  2. Current residents read your essays

    Only your essay answers go to the Selection Committee, made up of current Bowles students. They never see demographic or background details.

  3. Scores are averaged

    Each member scores responses on a set scale, and the averages decide Accepted or Waitlist status for each application period. You'll hear by email.

  4. Rooms are matched

    We try to meet single or double requests and use a “best match” method for roommates. All rooms are single sex.

Bowles supports the Fair Housing Act and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability or familial status. The waitlist is not ranked; admission from it weighs a balance of men and women, a mix of class years, and a range of backgrounds and interests.
