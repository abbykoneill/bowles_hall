Support the next generation

When the Bowles Hall project began in 2005, it was a small group of alumni determined to revive the residential college. In 2020, the first class of four-year residents graduated from the restored Hall.

Help us sustain the Bowles experience and give a new generation the programs and traditions that make it meaningful. The Bowles Hall Foundation is a 501(c)(3), and gifts are tax-deductible.
