Bowles Hall Foundation Board of Directors

Alumni, Berkeley faculty and campus leaders who steward the Hall on behalf of its residents.

Officers, 2026–2027

Scott Wilson

President

Scott received BA degrees in Political Science and German from Cal in 1985 and a PhD in Political Science from UC Santa Barbara in 1995. His career has focused on the regulatory and quality requirements of new medical devices, particularly the US and EU approval processes. He recently joined Cala Health, which is studying ways to address chronic disease by stimulating peripheral nerves with body-worn electronics. His earlier ventures include start-ups developing devices for hypertension, heart valve leakage, therapeutic hypothermia and heart failure, as well as work with Medtronic and Edwards Lifesciences. Scott is also a member of the Rotary Club of Walnut Creek.

Leslie Peek

Vice President

Leslie is a Senior Career Advisor at the UC Davis Internship and Career Center, after more than ten years in residential education at Cal and UC Davis. She earned a BS in Psychology from UC Davis and a master's in Student Affairs from the University of Delaware. As Resident Director for Bowles and Stern, she created a mentoring program that let returning students build leadership skills by mentoring first-year residents. She became an Academic Coordinator in Student Housing at UC Davis in 2011 and moved to the UC Davis ICC in 2015.

Lawrence Jones

Chief Financial Officer

Larry holds a bachelor's degree in Economics and Corporate Finance from UC Berkeley and a master's in corporate finance from UCLA. He spent more than 35 years at PwC, including 18 months in Venezuela and leading the firm's real estate investment trust (REIT) practice, and served as an accounting fellow in the SEC's Office of the Chief Accountant. Since retiring in 2007 he has served on several boards, including as audit committee chair for Cole Credit Property Trust IV and on the City of Dallas Park & Recreation Board. He is a licensed CPA in California.

Isaac Jackson

Secretary / Counsel

Isaac is a Prosecuting Attorney with the San Diego County District Attorney's Office. He received his BA from UC Berkeley in 2012, double majoring in Political Science and Rhetoric, and his JD from UC Hastings in 2016. At Cal he was a Bowles resident, Resident Assistant and Program Coordinator. He has been involved with the Bowles Hall project since 2012 and joined the Foundation's Board in 2014.

John Baker

President Emeritus

John has practiced civil engineering for more than 40 years. After earning his bachelor's and master's in Civil Engineering at UC Berkeley, he served as a Lieutenant in the U.S. Navy Civil Engineer Corps, then held positions at PG&E, the California Department of General Services, and private engineering firms, including many years as a Vice President with the Anderson Consulting Group. He finished his career as Senior Vice President at Kleinfelder, where he helped establish the James H. Kleinfelder Fellowship in Geotechnical Engineering for Berkeley graduates.
