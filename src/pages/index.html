Why Bowles

A small college inside a big university.

Berkeley is enormous. Bowles is a home for 188 students who eat together, run their own traditions, and have faculty mentors under the same roof.

How the college works

  1. America's first residential college

    Built on the Oxford and Cambridge model and opened in 1929, Bowles is the only residential college at Berkeley.

  2. Faculty down the hall

    The College Principal and Dean are Berkeley faculty who live and dine in the Hall, advising you from first semester to graduation.

  3. A home for all four years

    Stay from first year through senior year. Returning residents get priority in room selection.

  4. Meals included, dining together

    Every room has its own bathroom and a full meal plan, and Bowles counts as on-campus housing for financial aid.

  5. Run by students

    Residents elect their own government, read applications on the Selection Committee, and have a seat on the Foundation's Board.

  6. Traditions and teams

    Yule Ball, Founders Ball, the Haunted House, Big Game Dinner, and more intramural teams than any other organization at Berkeley.

Bowles History

Nearly a century on the hill.

Founded in 1928 through a gift from Mary McNear Bowles in memory of her husband, Regent Philip E. Bowles, the Hall opened in 1929. Its motto, “Education Through Fellowship,” guided more than 6,000 residents. After an alumni-led, privately funded restoration, it reopened in 2016 as a residential college once more.

All FAQs
What is a residential college?

A permanent, faculty-led residence that gives students the advantages of a small community inside a large university, a model that began at Oxford in 1249. Students run the social and cultural life, with faculty living alongside them. Think of it as a home away from home.

Who can apply?

Incoming first-year students, transfer students and continuing Berkeley students of every year and major. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. How to apply.

How much does it cost, and what's included?

For 2025–2026, a single is $14,250 and a double $13,375 per semester, payable monthly. Every room has an attached bathroom, and the full meal plan is included. Rooms & pricing.

How are residents chosen?

Current Bowles students on the Selection Committee score your essay answers without seeing any demographic or background details.

Is Bowles run by Cal Housing? Can I still join Greek life?

Bowles is operated by the non-profit Bowles Hall Foundation in partnership with UC Berkeley, and counts as on-campus housing for financial aid. Residents are encouraged to join groups across campus, including Greek life.
