Student Life · Rooms & Amenities

A historic hall, restored for today

After earning a place on the National Register of Historic Places in 1989, Bowles was restored inch by inch over 14 months, from June 2015 to August 2016.

What's in the Hall

  • 42 single and 73 double rooms, each with an attached bathroom. No triples or quads.
  • On-site dining for all meals, plus an evening study break
  • The historic Hart Memorial Library and 7 study rooms with whiteboards and supplies
  • A large lounge with a grand piano and a 150" theater with surround sound
  • Game room with billiards, ping-pong and more
  • Student kitchen and community garden
  • High-speed wifi throughout, plus cable TV
  • Washers, dryers and printers throughout the building
  • Noise-reducing walls and floors
  • Seismic retrofitting and security that exceed current standards
